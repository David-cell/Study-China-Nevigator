Study in China: Application Timeline & Deadlines

Plan 6 to 9 months ahead for the September intake.

Most programs start in September

The main intake is autumn (September). Applications usually open October to December and close February to April (a few run to May). Aim to submit 6 to 9 months before you want to arrive.

Month-by-month (indicative)

Oct to Dec
University applications open; some autumn CSC and MOFCOM rounds open.
Nov to Mar
University-postgraduate and Excellence CSC nominations.
Jan to Apr
Bilateral CSC via your home-country Chinese embassy (the largest window).
Feb to Apr
Most degree-program deadlines.
Apr to Jul
Provincial and university scholarships; admission notices sent.
Jul to Aug
Visa (X1/X2) and pre-departure.

Two things to watch

  • CSCA aptitude exam: from the 2026/27 cycle, most undergraduate applicants sit this exam. See the CSCA guide.
  • Dates shift yearly: always confirm the exact window on Campus China and your target university's admission page.
