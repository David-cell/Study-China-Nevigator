Southern Medical University

南方医科大学
GuangzhouMedical省属重点

Leading medical university in Guangzhou, well known for its English-taught MBBS program.

At a glance

Location
Guangzhou, South China
Degree levels
Undergraduate · Graduate (Master's / PhD)
English-taught
MBBS is fully English-taught; other programs are Chinese-taught (HSK required).
CSCA aptitude exam
Required for undergraduate applicants (national policy, from 2026/27). — subjects: 理科中文, 数学, 化学
Language requirement
HSK for Chinese-taught; IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80 for English-taught (MBBS).
Application deadline
Self-funded: Dec 1 – Jun 30.
Tuition (indicative)
¥30,000–45,000/yr (medical programs); confirm on istudy.smu.edu.cn.

Strong subjects

  • Clinical Medicine
  • Basic Medicine
  • Biomedical Engineering

Why students choose it

  • Heritage of the former First Military Medical University
  • Strong clinical medicine
  • Located in Guangzhou

Highlights

  • International Student Service System (fimmu.at0086.cn) for online application.
  • English-medium MBBS listed by China's Ministry of Education.
  • International Education College manages admissions and scholarships.
