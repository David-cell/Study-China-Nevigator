Central China Normal University

华中师范大学
WuhanEducationNormal211

211/Double First-Class normal university in Wuhan; a national base for international Chinese education and teacher training.

At a glance

Location
Wuhan, Central China
Degree levels
Undergraduate · Graduate (Master's / PhD)
English-taught
Some English-taught graduate programs; many Chinese-taught. Chinese Language programs at multiple HSK levels.
CSCA aptitude exam
Required for undergraduate applicants (national policy, from 2026/27). — subjects: 文科中文, 数学
Language requirement
HSK 4–5 by program for Chinese-taught; English proof for English-taught.
Application deadline
Fall: Mar 1 – Jul 15; Spring (language only): Oct 1 – Jan 15.
Tuition (indicative)
Per program; Chinese language short courses ~¥2,200; degree tuition on request.

  • Psychology

Why students choose it

  • Top-tier teacher education

  • Located in Wuhan

Highlights

  • 211 & Double First-Class normal university
  • National base for Chinese language education
  • Wuhan location, ~2,750 international students
