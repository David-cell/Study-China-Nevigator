How to apply

  1. Check eligibility and the language requirement (HSK for Chinese-taught programs, or an English test for English-taught programs).
  2. Prepare your documents: notarised transcripts, a study plan, two recommendation letters, and the physical-examination form.
  3. Submit through the official portal below, and sit the CSCA aptitude assessment if you are applying for undergraduate admission.
  4. Track your deadline and wait for the admission notice; apply for a CSC scholarship separately if you are eligible.
