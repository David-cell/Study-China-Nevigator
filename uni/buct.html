At a glance

Location
Beijing, North China
Degree levels
Undergraduate · Graduate (Master's / PhD)
English-taught
Many master's and PhD programs are English-taught (Chemical Engineering, Materials, etc.); Chinese-taught programs require HSK 4.
CSCA aptitude exam
Required for undergraduate applicants (national policy, from 2026/27). — subjects: 理科中文, 数学, 物理
Language requirement
HSK 4 (score ≥180) for Chinese-taught; TOEFL ≥85 / IELTS ≥6.0 for English-taught.
Application deadline
Degree programs: Nov – Jun (2026 deadline 30 Jun).
Tuition (indicative)

Highlights
